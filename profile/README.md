# Nielson Zoltan

**Embedded systems engineering in St. George, Utah.**

We're an external engineering team for companies that need embedded products designed, fixed or brought back to life: hands-on engineering that solves the problem, without the buzzwords.

## What we do

- **Embedded systems design**: firmware, PCB design, IoT hardware, sensors and wireless communication, from first prototype through testing.
- **Product reverse engineering**: analyse, document and rebuild existing hardware and firmware.
- **Product redesign**: replace obsolete parts, improve reliability and modernise products that are still selling.

Alongside that: firmware and hardware debugging, PCB modifications, fine-pitch soldering and rework, prototyping and bench testing, and 3D-printed housings and fixtures.

We've worked in medical, mobility, gaming, consumer electronics and power management for commercial transportation, with more than 40 years of combined experience across the team. Mentoring and teaching young engineers is part of how we work.
